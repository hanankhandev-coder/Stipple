Four rules on one card

  1. 01
    Grade

    Set the look once: contrast, warmth and how much colour. Every photo is graded to match, whoever shot it and on whatever camera.

  2. 02
    Crop

    Pick your ratios and where the subject sits. Stipple finds the person in each shot and crops around them, for every size you export.

  3. 03
    Type

    Your wordmark and fonts land in the same corner on every export, scaled right for a story frame or a three-metre window.

  4. 04
    Colour

    One brand colour, used the same way every time: a band, a block or a full field. No more six shades of almost-blue.

Questions, answered

What is a Style Card?

One page that holds your brand's photo rules: grade, crop, type and colour. Stipple reads it every time anyone on your team exports a photo, so nobody has to remember the brand book.

Do I need to be a designer?

No. Upload ten photos you love, or answer six questions about your brand, and Stipple drafts the card. A designer can fine-tune it later.

Which sizes can it export?

Every common social, web and print size out of the box: 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9 and A-series posters, plus any custom size you add to the card.

Does it change my original photos?

Never. Originals stay untouched. Stipple makes new exports and keeps a link back to the source, so you can re-run them if the card changes.

Can freelancers and agencies use our card?

Yes. Send a link and they export through your card without seeing the rest of your workspace. Studio plans include five seats.

Where does it plug in?

Use the web app, the Figma and Lightroom plugins, or the API. Exports can go straight to your shop, your CMS or a shared folder.
